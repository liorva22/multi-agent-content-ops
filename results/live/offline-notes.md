# Offline Mode Is Coming to Notes: Your Notes, With or Without a Connection

**TL;DR:** Offline Mode lets mobile users read, create, and edit notes without a connection, and launches in app version 5.0 on June 10.

## Why we built it

Field technicians and travelers often lose work when connectivity drops. Our app reviews made the problem clear: 23% of one-star reviews in the last year mention losing a note offline. We built Offline Mode to solve it.

## How it works

Notes are stored locally in an encrypted on-device database. Edits made offline are queued and merged when the device reconnects.

Sometimes the same note is edited on two devices. In that case, we keep both versions and you choose one. Nothing is silently overwritten.

## What we saw in the beta

The beta ran for 8 weeks and included 2,400 users. Sync conflicts occurred in 0.6% of synced notes. The beta produced zero data-loss reports. Weekly active usage among beta users rose 12% compared with a control group.

## Availability

Offline Mode ships in app version 5.0. It launches on June 10 on iOS and Android, and it is available for all plans.

Offline storage is capped at 2 GB per device.

## What Offline Mode doesn't do

Two things are outside the scope of Offline Mode. It does not support real-time offline collaboration. It also does not support offline search across attachments.

## Get ready for June 10

If you have ever lost a note because your connection dropped, Offline Mode is built for that situation. You can read, create, and edit notes without a connection. Your changes sync automatically when you reconnect. Update to version 5.0 when it arrives on June 10.
