# PRD: Usage-Based Billing Dashboard

## Summary
The Usage Dashboard shows customers, in near real time, what they are consuming and what their next invoice will be, so finance teams are never surprised by a bill.

## Problem
Since we moved to usage-based pricing, billing-related support tickets have tripled, and invoice disputes now take an average of 11 days to resolve.

## Solution
A dashboard inside the admin console shows daily usage per product, a month-to-date cost, and a forecast of the month-end invoice. Admins can set budget alerts at any amount and receive an email when 80% and 100% of the budget is reached.

## Beta results
- 60 enterprise accounts used the dashboard for one billing cycle.
- The month-end forecast was within 5% of the final invoice for 52 of the 60 accounts.
- Billing-related tickets from beta accounts fell by 38%.

## Launch
Generally available on September 15 for all Enterprise customers. Usage data refreshes every 15 minutes.

## Non-goals
Changing prices or plans from the dashboard. Multi-currency invoices.
